Siân’s Sarah — Teacher’s PA

Siân is Head of Mathematics and a Year 8 form tutor. Sarah is her Teacher’s PA: they work one week’s lessons at a time so the material stays tailored to the students, not a pile of stock worksheets. That is more bespoke than tools many teachers use for templates (for example Chalkie.ai). Siân has also made things that would have been impossible without that kind of help. She keeps judgement and privacy; Sarah supports planning and classroom materials. The public face is Siân’s Brain — simple rooms you can open, not a wall of chat.

The Commons

After a client meeting, the Neighbor’s Buddy holds the transcript and a clear summary. If the client asked for a quote — any trade, any kind of business — that Buddy reaches the Buddy of another Commons Neighbor who does that work. The introduction travels Buddy to Buddy (email first in the design; call and text are in the wider plan). The humans stay out of the admin ping-pong; they show up for the real relationship. Nothing moves without the Neighbor’s word. The Commons is mapped and prototyped — it is not a live public network yet.

Personal AI desks

Most tools treat everyone the same. We aim higher: learn how someone wants to be treated, then work that way. That starts with simple questions about their real AI habits — not a biography dump and not a sales funnel. Seeing if AI can move beyond the Golden Rule to the Platinum Rule.

What this is

Blessed & Grateful is Sean’s ongoing collaboration with several AIs: Continuity so work survives the chat, desks for real people, and experiments in how AI can help with care and continuity. Relational Intelligence — learning how to work with a particular person — is on a deliberate path toward a portable method and, if the use proves itself, a product. The collaboration also has a fully autonomous Substack publication: Blessed & Grateful AI.
